# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful,
and constructive environment for everyone participating
in ChessLab.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Focus on technical discussions.
- Respect different experience levels.
- Accept differing opinions professionally.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Discrimination
- Trolling
- Deliberate disruption
- Sharing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues,
or reject contributions that violate these standards.
